# Green Light

A private **Yes / Maybe / No** list for two people.

Answer on your own device, exchange a small share file, and only see what you **both** marked Yes—including complementary give/receive pairs (e.g. giving oral ↔ receiving oral). Nothing is uploaded to a server. Everything stays in the browser on your device.

**Adult content.** For consenting adults only. A Yes here is a conversation starter, not a commitment. Either person can change their mind at any time.

---

## Features

- **~200 topics** from everyday intimacy through more adventurous / kink-aware options
- **Umbrella questions** — for heavier areas, answer Yes or Maybe on a gate question to unlock specifics; answer No to skip the whole branch
- **Complementary matching** — if one of you wants to *give* and the other wants to *receive*, it counts as a mutual Yes
- **Skip = No** — unanswered questions are treated as No in your share file and in comparisons
- **Categories** as collapsible sections (first open by default; rest closed)
- **Custom topics** — add your own, including optional give/receive pairs
- **Local-only** — answers saved in this browser; export a share file to compare with a partner
- **Works on phone and desktop** — open the HTML file in any modern browser

## How to use

### Solo (your answers)
1. Open `index.html` (or the hosted page) in your browser.
2. Optionally set a label under **Your Info**.
3. Work through **Answer topics**. Use Yes / Maybe / No, or skip (skip = No).
4. For sections with an **unlocks more** gate: Yes or Maybe reveals sub-options; No keeps them hidden.

### Together (compare)
1. Each person answers on their **own** device.
2. **Export share file** (JSON).
3. Send that file to your partner (Messages, email, AirDrop, etc.).
4. Under **Compare privately**, import their file.
5. Review:
   - **Mutual Yes** — both said Yes (including complementary pairs)
   - **Worth discussing** — involves at least one Maybe
   - **Different answers** (optional checkbox) — Yes vs No differences

Results **never label who answered what**. The share file itself is readable plain JSON—only share it with the person you intend to share with.

## Privacy

- No accounts, no backend, no analytics built into the app.
- Answers live in **browser local storage** on that device.
- Clearing site data, switching browsers, or using private/incognito mode can erase local answers—use **Export share file** or **Download full personal backup** if you want a copy.
- The share file includes Yes / Maybe / No for topics (skipped → No) so matches can be calculated. It is **not encrypted**.

## Mobile

1. Save or download `index.html` to the phone (Files, Drive, email).
2. Open in Safari or Chrome.
3. Export/import share files through the usual share sheet.

On iPhone, “Open in Safari” from the Files app is often the most reliable for local HTML.

---

## Custom topics

Under **Add a topic**:
- Pick an existing category, or **Other** to name a new one.
- Check **give / receive pair** to add two linked topics that match across roles.

Custom topics and pairs are included in the share file so your partner can answer them too.
